What this prototype demonstrates

From the planShown here
Tier B rendererReal-time WebGL2, instanced glyph quads. 0 bytes of media above the fold — the whole hero is math.
Scrub travelDesktop 400% of viewport height, mobile 260% — the two devices are at the same scroll fraction but deliberately different distances.
Act II dolly-zoomFOV widens 50°→75° while the camera pushes in; glyphs velocity-stretch into trails, then a shockwave ripples out on deceleration.
Act III resolveThe cylinder separates into six constellations, one per discipline — a 3-column grid on desktop, a vertical stack on mobile.
Act IV handoffThe six constellations settle into the six service cards instead of hard-cutting to the next section.
Unit fixEvery height is resolved from the device box, not vh — the bug that makes the live site jump when the URL bar hides.
Tier AToggle it in the rail: animation stops, copy still reveals. What prefers-reduced-motion and Save-Data visitors get.

Two independent WebGL contexts run side by side, driven by one scrub, so the desktop and mobile choreography can be compared at identical scroll fractions — that divergence is the argument of §5.3 of the plan, made visible.
